  • Dark (metallic wordmark) belongs on KIT Navy and Deep Charcoal. Light (tonal wordmark) belongs on white, KIT Paper and Light Grey. Never swap them.
  • Mono white for KIT Blue, photography and single-pass print; mono navy for one-colour print, engraving and etching. Below 20 mm use monochrome.
  • Minimum size: full lockup 32 mm / 130 px; wordmark 14 mm / 56 px. App icons and favicons use the wordmark only, never the lockup.
  • Clear space is 1H (the cap height) on all sides. Gold is never a logo ground. Do not recolour, stretch, outline or rearrange.
